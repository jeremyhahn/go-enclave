# Plan: a fuzz target for the TPM sealed-blob parser (#336)

## Objective

`backends/tpm` parses blobs read back from storage (`parseBlob`, `sized`,
`checkPublic` in `backends/tpm/blob.go`) before any TPM command runs. The
table tests in `blob_test.go` cover chosen inputs; `FuzzParseBlob` holds the
parser to its contract on arbitrary input, with no TPM and no swtpm.

## Slices

1. Test: `backends/tpm/blob_fuzz_test.go` with `FuzzParseBlob`, seeded with
   the well-formed test blob, each of its truncations, a wrong version byte,
   a trailing byte, a private area at and one past `maxPrivateBytes`, and the
   public area with one template field changed. On every input:
   - no panic;
   - a refusal matches `ErrSealedAuthentication`;
   - an accepted blob re-encodes byte for byte (`encodeBlob(public, private)`
     equals the input), its public area is the sealed-object template with a
     32-byte unique, its private area is at most `maxPrivateBytes`, and both
     areas alias the input at their framed offsets;
   - the input is unchanged.
2. Fix (only if the fuzzer finds a break): the crasher under
   `backends/tpm/testdata/fuzz/FuzzParseBlob` as a failing test commit, then
   the fix in `blob.go`.
3. Changelog fragment `changelog.d/336-tpm-blob-fuzz.md`.

## Out of scope

- Fuzzing `Unprotect` against a TPM or swtpm (needs a device).
- Any change to the blob format or the root module.
