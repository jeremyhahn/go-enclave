# Plan: confidential-vm (#194)

## Objective

SPEC row `confidential-vm`, designed in
[docs/backends/confidential-vm.md](../backends/confidential-vm.md): a
confidential VM is not a backend. The row ships a standalone `Attester`
whose evidence is the SEV-SNP report or the TD quote, read through Linux
configfs-tsm, and on SEV-SNP a `KeyProtector` keyed by the SNP derived key,
bound to the guest's measurement. TDX has no derived sealing key. Proven on
the real part only.

## Slices

1. **Design (done, docs only).** Host record, proposed answers, SPEC row,
   changelog fragment.
2. **amd-sev module (done).** `backends/amdsev`: report parser,
   configfs-tsm client and probe, `New`, `Attest`, `Import`, `Sweep`, typed
   errors, off-platform stub. Tests: `make test-backends`.
3. **amd-sev guest kit (done).** `scripts/amdsev-guest.sh`,
   `scripts/amdsev-host-scan.sh` and `scripts/amdsev-hostscan`. Tests: the
   buildgates dry-run and refusal tests, the helper's tests, lint-scripts.
4. **SNP derived key (done).** `SNP_GET_DERIVED_KEY` through
   `/dev/sev-guest`, received in locked memory, wiped and released on every
   path. Tests: struct codecs, refusal off an SNP guest, wipe and release.
5. **intel-tdx module (done, PR #354 folded).** `backends/inteltdx`:
   probe, `Attest` over configfs-tsm, `Import`, the native `Report`,
   `Measurements` and `ExtendRTMR`. Tests: `make test-backends`.
6. **Standalone Attester (done).** `backends/confidentialvm`:
   `NewAttester` reads the configfs-tsm provider (`sev_guest` or
   `tdx_guest`); `Attest` returns `sev-snp-report` or `tdx-quote` evidence
   from an entry of its own, checking provider and generation; typed
   `*TSMReportError`; off-platform stub; the root `Attester` doc comment
   gains its third placement. Tests: nonce layout, provider mapping,
   generation, each refused step against a real directory, the stub.
7. **SNP KeyProtector (next).** `NewKeyProtector` for `ModePerOperation`
   keyed by the derived key, moved from `backends/amdsev`, with the blob of
   the design's "SEV-SNP derived-key KeyProtector" and
   `*DerivedKeyError` matching `ErrDerivedKeyUnavailable` (in a TDX guest
   too). Tests: blob codec and tamper, off-platform refusals, the
   providertest suite where it runs without the device.
8. **Drop the backend drafts.** Remove `backends/amdsev` and
   `backends/inteltdx` (their `Import`, `Sweep`, native and backend
   surface), keep the guest kit pointing at the new module, and fold their
   changelog entries into the new module's.
9. **Ship the row.** Mark `confidential-vm` shipped in docs/SPEC.md and
   ROADMAP.md once 6 to 8 hold.
10. **Hardware runs (blocked on the hosts).** SNP host and TDX host: the
   design's test plan in a real guest.

## Out of scope

- A simulator, emulator or mock device.
- Registering anything at import: `main` calls `Register`.
- Any root-module change beyond the `Attester` doc comment.
