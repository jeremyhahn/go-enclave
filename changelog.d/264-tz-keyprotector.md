### Added

- **The `arm-trustzone` backend module has a TrustZone `KeyProtector`**: `(*Backend).KeyProtector()` returns an `enclave.KeyProtector` and `enclave.HealthReporter` that seals keys with the OP-TEE trusted application's SEAL command and opens them with UNSEAL, under a fixed associated-data label; a tampered blob or a length mismatch fails with `enclave.ErrSealedAuthentication`.
- **The TrustZone sealing transport is tested without OP-TEE, and `GO_ENCLAVE_OPTEE_SEAL=required` makes SEAL mandatory on the fixture**: an ordinary `go test` runs SEAL and UNSEAL through the real client against a fake TEE driver; on the OP-TEE fixture the KeyProtector test skips a missing SEAL only when the fixture declares, as `GO_ENCLAVE_OPTEE_SEAL=absent:<release>`, that its pinned trusted-firmware release lacks SEAL, and any other SEAL error fails it, and `make integration-test-arm-trustzone GO_ENCLAVE_OPTEE_SEAL=required` fails, rather than skips, when the trusted application has no SEAL.
