### Added

- **test: the arm-trustzone harness runs in trusted-firmware's OP-TEE fixture, pinned at `v0.4.2-alpha`.** `scripts/tool-pins.sh` pins `TRUSTED_FIRMWARE_TAG` with `TRUSTED_FIRMWARE_COMMIT`, the commit the tag peels to; `scripts/optee-fixture.sh` clones the tag outside the tree (`~/.cache/go-enclave/trusted-firmware/<tag>`, URL from the caller's `TRUSTED_FIRMWARE_URL`) and refuses a checkout at any other commit, so a moved tag is never followed. `make optee-fixture-check` boots the fixture and runs its own xtest and trusted-application check, with no go-enclave code. `make integration-test-arm-trustzone` builds the `optee`-tagged harness as a CGO-free linux/arm64 test binary and runs it in the fixture's guest through `make optee-run`, with `GO_ENCLAVE_OPTEE_ENDPOINT` defaulting to `/dev/tee0`. The binary is compiled in the toolchain image, and its `optee`-tagged `TestMain` refuses unless the endpoint exists, while every other build keeps the devcontainer guard. The CI job that runs these targets waits on #206 slice 2. Nothing changes for callers of the `enclave` package.
